ApplAI's Polisher provides a Perplexity skill to **convert the priviously [composed](../S03_COMPOSER/_ApplAI%20Composer.md) and then manually finalized "SHORT-CV" and "COVER-LETTER" from *.md format into well formatted, modern looking PDF-files to impress recruiter.  

After syncing your remote GitHub with your local Repo, you will find the generated "Short-CV" and "Cover-Letter" files in *.pdf format, ready for being uploaded to the job-application-portal. 

## How it works

> 1. Open a new **Perplexity-Prompt**
> 2. type "**Polish JobApplication**"
> 3. **Follow the instructions**
> 5. Finally **Git-sync** your local ["jobs" name JobOffers Repo](../../../../jobs/JOBS/_JobOffers.md) to find the generated files in *.pdf format for uploading in the *\JOBS\Company-Role-YYMM\APPLICATION*-folder.
