# Composer Skill

-> [JobSuche](../../../../JobSuche/_JobSuche.md)

---

ApplAI's Composer is a Perplexity skill to **compile a most promising CV and Coverletter** from 

1. The previously [jscraped](../S01_SCRAPER/_ApplAI_JobScraper.md) **JobOffer** files. 

2. The [Reactive Resume](../../../../JobSuche/06_TOOLS/R/Reactive%20Resume/_Reactive%20Resume.md) managed **[SuperCV](../S02_GENERATOR/VSC/data/SuperCV/_SuperCVs.md)** Perplexity already knows. 

After syncing your remote GitHub with your local Repo, you will find the generated "Short-CV" and "Cover-Letter" files in *.md format for manual editing in your *\JOBS\Company-Role-YYMM\APPLICATION*-folder. 

## How to compose

> 1. Open a new **Perplexity-Prompt**
> 2. type "**Compose**"
> 3. **Follow the instructions**
> 5. Finally **Git-sync** your local ["jobs" name JobOffers Repo](../../../../jobs/JOBS/_JobOffers.md) to find the generated files in *.md format for manual editing in your *\JOBS\Company-Role-YYMM\APPLICATION*-folder.
