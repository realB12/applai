# HR Open Standards Organization

-> **[CV DataCollection](../../04%20MODEL/02%20DATA/CV/_CV_DataCollection.md)**: my live CV DATA

* **[JSON Resume](../J/JSON%20Resume.md)**: an alternative, ways simpler standard

The HR Open Standards Organization is an ambitious plan to **put everything HR on an API**, including screening, benefits, and payroll. 

Currently this includes "The Learning Employment Record (LER-RS)"

Its focus is on skills and competencies, versus job titles and duties.

Digital verification of credentials and achievements.
The resume-like elements are in the Person Profile type, which resides in the Candidate type.

The schema has roughly 350 high-level elements, and nests down 28 levels.
